Contact

Reach the Foundation with a clear purpose and a safe channel.

URAI Foundation welcomes thoughtful inquiries from researchers, nonprofits, accessibility advocates, community partners, reviewers, volunteers, and supporters.

General inquiries

For mission, programs, education, governance, or public-interest collaboration.

hello@uraifoundation.org

Partnerships

For nonprofit, research, accessibility, education, or community partnership discussions.

partners@uraifoundation.org

Accessibility feedback

For barriers, readability concerns, compatibility issues, or accessibility collaboration.

accessibility@uraifoundation.org

Privacy questions

For questions about website privacy, data handling, consent, or public-interest governance records.

privacy@uraifoundation.org

Security reports

For sensitive security, privacy, safety, abuse, or vulnerability concerns. Do not include sensitive details in public issues.

security@uraifoundation.org

Volunteer or support interest

For people interested in contributing expertise, reviewing standards, supporting public education, or helping with community work.

support@uraifoundation.org

Public collaboration

Use public issues for non-sensitive standards feedback.

Public governance feedback, documentation improvements, and standards discussions may be opened in the public repository when they do not contain private, sensitive, or security-relevant information.
