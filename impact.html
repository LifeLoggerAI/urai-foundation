Impact

Measure progress by whether people are safer, clearer, and better informed.

URAI Foundation prioritizes practical public benefit over inflated metrics. The goal is to improve accountability, accessibility, understanding, and trust around human-centered AI systems.

Accessibility

Encourage systems that work for people with different abilities, languages, literacy levels, neurotypes, and sensory needs.

Transparency

Publish standards, review practices, limitations, and governance expectations in language that non-specialists can understand.

Education

Create public-interest explainers and educational resources about privacy, consent, explainability, and responsible AI use.

Community trust

Support review practices that involve affected communities rather than treating ethics as an afterthought.

Privacy-first guidance

Encourage minimization, consent, transparency, and safer defaults for systems handling sensitive information.

Open standards

Maintain public governance materials that can be reviewed, criticized, improved, and reused.

No inflated claims

Credibility matters more than spectacle.

The Foundation should avoid exaggerated promises about artificial intelligence, mental health, prediction, or human understanding. Public trust is built through careful language, reviewable evidence, and responsible boundaries.

Impact reporting should distinguish goals, experiments, proposals, published standards, and verified outcomes.
