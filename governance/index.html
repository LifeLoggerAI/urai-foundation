Principles

The public layer follows four rules.

01

Evidence before claim

Entity status, programs, partnerships, financial facts, fundraising authority, and outcomes are stated only when current evidence supports them.

02

Role before authority

A title, domain, or institutional relationship does not create legal, financial, operational, or data-access authority.

03

Correction before concealment

Material public inaccuracies receive a visible correction path rather than silent institutional drift.

04

Public before protected

Public governance explains principles and boundaries. Admin, provider, security, and operational controls remain protected.

Institutional separation
