URAI capabilities can compose around an objective. Identity, privacy, permission, provenance, and evidence remain between intent and action. The diagram is architectural—not a claim that every integration is currently deployed.

Accessibility as infrastructure

Access cannot be a page added after the product is finished.

URAI’s accessibility model treats alternative input, semantic structure, reduced stimulation, captions, transcripts, screen-reader paths, directional audio, haptics, and low-motion equivalents as system behaviors.

◉

Visual & Deaf access

Captions, visible state, haptic alternatives, transcripts, and communication patterns that do not depend on sound.

⌁

Blind & low-vision access

Semantic structure, TTS-compatible content, directional audio concepts, robust focus, and non-canvas parity.

≈

Low-stimulation access

Reduced motion, controlled density, predictable transitions, and calmer equivalents for immersive experiences.

↳

Cognitive clarity

Plain-language permissions, recoverable flows, explicit consequences, and progressive disclosure of complexity.
