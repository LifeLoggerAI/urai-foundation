URAI System Atlas · Foundation lens

The Foundation can use the machinery without owning the machinery.

URAI composes capabilities around an objective. Identity, privacy, permission, provenance, and evidence remain between intent and action. This diagram describes the architecture; it does not claim every integration is deployed or available to the public.

  1. 01
    Need

    A real accessibility, education, or community problem enters the institution.

  2. 02
    Compose

    Appropriate research, content, creative, spatial, and analytical capabilities are selected.

  3. 03
    Authorize

    Purpose, identity, privacy, and permission boundaries determine what may happen.

  4. 04
    Evidence

    Outcomes return as inspectable evidence instead of unsupported institutional claims.

Accessibility demonstrated

The site itself should model the standard.

Readable contrast, keyboard navigation, strong focus, reduced-motion support, semantic structure, large targets, and low-stimulation presentation are part of the public experience—not a compliance footnote.

  • Visible focus and 44px targets
