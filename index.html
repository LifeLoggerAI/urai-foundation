Human-centered AI standards

Technology should preserve human dignity, context, and choice.

URAI Foundation develops public standards for systems that touch memory, identity, relationships, accessibility, and community—so important decisions can be understood, reviewed, and challenged.

Our north star

Build AI that strengthens agency and connection without turning a person’s life into material they cannot control.

Principles

Standards people can actually hold systems to.

The Foundation’s work is organized around concrete expectations for builders, reviewers, and the public.

Responsible AI

Expectations for consent, privacy, safety, explainability, escalation, and accountable use.

Accessibility

Inclusive design principles that reduce cognitive, sensory, and interaction barriers without claiming certification.

Emotional wellness

Boundaries for emotionally sensitive technology that distinguish support from diagnosis, treatment, or crisis care.

Deaf community

Design considerations for visual, haptic, contextual, and communication access without claiming community representation.

Research

Principles for evidence, uncertainty, participant dignity, data rights, and responsible publication.

Collaboration

A path for organizations and researchers interested in strengthening the standards without implying an existing partnership.

Open standards

The work should be inspectable.

Core governance documents remain public so commitments can be reviewed against the systems they are meant to guide.

Governance Charter

Decision structure, review categories, responsibilities, and escalation paths.

Ethical AI Principles

Baseline commitments to dignity, agency, fairness, safety, accountability, and review.

Transparency Framework

Disclosure, documentation, reporting, and incident-transparency practices.

Risk Review Process

A repeatable path for intake, classification, mitigation, approval, monitoring, and reassessment.

Decision Record

A reusable format for documenting consequential governance choices.

Risk Review Record

A practical structure for documenting identified risks, controls, evidence, and review.
