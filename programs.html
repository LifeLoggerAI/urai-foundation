Programs

Practical public-interest work, not vague AI promises.

URAI Foundation programs translate human-centered AI values into standards, education, accessibility practices, and reviewable accountability tools.

Responsible AI Standards

Open governance, risk-review, transparency, and accountability practices for systems that affect memory, identity, wellbeing, relationships, accessibility, or public trust.

Accessibility and Inclusion

Guidance for building AI experiences that are usable by people with different abilities, sensory needs, neurotypes, languages, literacy levels, and access constraints.

Public Education

Plain-English explainers, workshops, and materials that help communities understand AI capabilities, limitations, privacy tradeoffs, and rights-respecting design.

Community Accountability

Review practices that invite affected communities, researchers, nonprofits, and builders to identify risks, improve language, and strengthen safeguards before harm occurs.

How support helps

Funding should map to visible public benefit.

Support may help fund open standards, accessibility reviews, educational materials, public documentation, community listening sessions, privacy-first research, and responsible AI governance templates.

The Foundation should not claim tax deductibility or charitable status until the applicable legal or fiscal-sponsorship status is finalized and reviewed.
