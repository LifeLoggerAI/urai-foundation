Formation-stage methodology · privacy · public interest

Global Emotional Weather must help communities without exposing people.

The proposed URAI capability combines voluntary privacy-protected population signals with corroborating public evidence to surface regions that may deserve human attention.

Authority boundary: This page is not an active research study, humanitarian partnership, emergency authority, validated famine/conflict/disease prediction system, or Foundation certification.

Claim boundary

Observation is not diagnosis. Change is not causation. Attention is not an emergency declaration.

Permitted interpretation

What the system may say

Observation

A sufficiently private regional aggregate shows a current Emotional Weather state or change.

Corroborated signal

The regional observation aligns with trustworthy external evidence within a compatible place and time window.

Humanitarian attention

Evidence may justify human investigation. The label does not declare an emergency or predict an outcome.

Individual boundary

No path from region to person

Public-interest outputs must not expose names, account IDs, pseudonyms, exact addresses or coordinates, movement trails, memories, transcripts, raw media, relationship records, raw health records, or individual emotional histories. Pseudonymization alone is not anonymization.

Consent

Public-good participation is a separate choice

Participation must be explicit, voluntary, purpose-specific, and revocable. It must not be bundled with personalization, passive context, location permission, sensitive inference, biometric processing, or monetization. Revocation must stop future contribution and remove eligible unreleased contributions where technically possible.

Release gate

Unsafe cells stay silent

Privacy

Large-enough cohorts, geographic suppression, repeated-query protection, bounded privacy budget, no individual drill-down, and independent kill switches.

Evidence

Freshness, provenance, uncertainty, method version, limitations, and multiple external evidence categories where the claim requires corroboration.

Human review

Consequential interpretation remains reviewable, correctable, auditable, and limited to the evidence actually available.

Evidence standard

Engineering proof is not humanitarian effectiveness

Exact-SHA code and repeatable automated tests may provide reproducible engineering evidence. They do not by themselves establish real-world humanitarian effectiveness, population-scale validity, causal prediction, fairness across countries and languages, independent review, production deployment, or public benefit.
