Research

Research begins with questions, evidence, and public accountability.

URAI Foundation uses research language to frame open standards, review methods, and public-interest questions across human-centered AI.

Status: This page does not claim active institutional partnerships, sponsored research, funded studies, public programs, or approved research protocols unless future evidence is added.

Inquiry path

For now, research interest is handled through public issues or mailto contact. No backend intake or persistence is implemented in this repository.

Open standards

Research work should improve standards, templates, and review practices that others can inspect.

Evidence quality

Claims should separate observations, assumptions, early hypotheses, and verified outcomes.

Safe collaboration

Future collaboration language should be added only when scope, evidence, permission, and public wording are confirmed.
