Research · evidence · participant dignity

Research begins with questions, evidence, and public accountability.

URAI Foundation uses research to frame public-interest questions, review methods, accessibility needs, uncertainty, and human-impact standards—not to manufacture institutional authority.

Authority boundary: This page does not claim active institutional partnerships, sponsored research, funded studies, public programs, approved research protocols, tax-exempt status, or participant recruitment unless current evidence supports those claims. Review the canonical public-status disclosure.

Research standard

Question → method → evidence → limitation → artifact → public consequence.

Open standards

Research should improve standards, templates, methods, and review practices that others can inspect and correct.

Evidence quality

Claims distinguish observations, assumptions, hypotheses, demonstrated results, and unresolved limitations.

Participant dignity

Future work involving people must make consent, purpose, data scope, withdrawal, accessibility, and downstream use understandable before participation.
