Privacy Policy

Privacy should be understandable, limited, and respectful.

This policy describes the privacy posture for the URAI Foundation public website and community-facing communications. It is written for clarity and should be reviewed as the Foundation's legal and operational structure evolves.

Scope

This policy applies to the public URAI Foundation website, public repository participation, and direct communications with the Foundation. It does not replace privacy policies for separate URAI Labs products, commercial services, or third-party platforms.

Information we may receive

The Foundation may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, organization, message content, contribution history, issue comments, partnership inquiries, volunteer interest, or support inquiries.

Website data

The current static website should avoid unnecessary tracking. If privacy-preserving analytics are added later, the site should disclose the provider, purpose, retention period, and whether cookies or cross-site identifiers are used.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, coordinate contributions, review partnership requests, improve public-interest materials, handle safety or security reports, and maintain responsible governance records.

What we do not intend to do

The Foundation should not sell personal information, use visitor data for behavioral advertising, or combine nonprofit inquiry information with commercial product marketing without clear notice and appropriate consent.

Sensitive topics

Because the Foundation may discuss AI, mental health, accessibility, memory, identity, consent, and privacy, sensitive personal information should not be submitted unless it is necessary for the specific inquiry and safe to share.

Third-party services

GitHub, email providers, donation platforms, form providers, analytics providers, and fiscal sponsors may have their own privacy policies. When those services are added, the Foundation should link to them and explain their role.

Retention

The Foundation should retain information only as long as needed for the purpose it was provided, legal obligations, governance records, security handling, or public accountability.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@uraifoundation.org. If that mailbox is not yet active, use hello@uraifoundation.org.

Last updated: May 17, 2026. This policy should be reviewed by counsel before major donation, analytics, CRM, or data-collection integrations are launched.
