Public accountability

Governance should remain understandable to non-specialists.

The Foundation aims to keep governance language readable, practical, and reviewable. Public trust depends on whether outside communities can understand how decisions are made, reviewed, challenged, and improved.

As the organization evolves, governance structures, advisory participation, and accountability mechanisms should be documented publicly where appropriate.
