Proposed outreach/research groups: veterans and trauma-aware access, Deaf/hard-of-hearing inclusion, older adults/caregivers, cognitive/neurodivergent and sensory/motor accessibility, emotional safety and public accountability. Program menu and advisor kit are design/policy materials, **not evidence** of active programs, institutional endorsements, clinical research or served beneficiaries.

Proposed funding models: grants, philanthropy, restricted programs, sponsorship, donated cloud/technical services, fiscal sponsorship and *future legally authorized* donations. Historic regional-profit distribution proposals, including user-described 15% community / 15% state / 15% national sharing of profits, are **commercial/revenue policy ideas only**, not approved Foundation funding, signed revenue-sharing covenants, actual payments or charitable contributions.
