# URAI Foundation Advisor Account Setup

## Purpose

This note tracks public-safe setup tasks for bringing a foundation advisor into URAI Foundation.

Do not publish a personal email address in this public repository.

## Advisor email options to discuss

Ask the advisor which organization email format she prefers.

Possible formats:

- first.last@uraifoundation.org
- initial.last@uraifoundation.org
- dr.last@uraifoundation.org
- first.last@urailabs.com
- initial.last@urailabs.com
- advisor@uraifoundation.org as a forwarding alias

Recommended default:

- first.last@uraifoundation.org for foundation-facing work
- first.last@urailabs.com only if she will also advise URAI Labs product/commercial work

## Suggested question

Which foundation email would you prefer us to create for you, and should it be a full inbox or a forwarding alias at first?

## Setup checklist

- Confirm exact public name and credential.
- Confirm preferred email handle.
- Confirm whether the account should be full inbox or forwarding alias.
- Confirm whether it is Foundation-only or Labs plus Foundation.
- Add advisor to internal contact list.
- Add advisor to private onboarding packet.
- Do not publish personal contact info in public docs.

## Access boundaries

Start with minimum access.

Give access only to:

- public foundation docs
- advisor packet
- claims and safety policy
- accessibility checklist
- program menu
- pilot planning documents

Do not grant access to private founder records, legal packets, medical records, or sensitive financial materials unless separately approved.
