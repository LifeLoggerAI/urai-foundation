# URAI Veteran and Accessibility Pathway

## Purpose

This document helps prepare the veteran, disability, and accessibility angle for URAI Foundation while keeping sensitive personal records protected.

## Key principle

Founder lived experience may support:

- credibility
- founder-market fit
- disability accessibility relevance
- veteran-centered mission
- warm introductions into veteran networks
- veteran entrepreneurship programs
- veteran-serving nonprofit or foundation partnerships

It should not be used to claim URAI is medically validated.

## Documents to gather privately

- benefit/status summary documents if the founder chooses to use them
- proof of veteran status if applicable
- short lived-experience statement
- why URAI matters for veterans and disabled people
- accessibility needs URAI is designed around
- why privacy and user control matter
- URAI Foundation mission one-pager
- advisor packet
- claims and safety policy
- accessibility checklist
- program menu
- screenshots or demo links
- repo/proof receipts
- market signal evidence

## Do not share broadly

- full medical records
- private benefit details
- legal packets
- private health details
- social security number
- addresses
- claim numbers
- unrelated medical information

Use redacted versions when possible.

## Suggested founder statement

I am building URAI from lived experience around memory, identity, emotional continuity, accessibility, and rebuilding life structure. I am not claiming URAI is a medical treatment. I am building a responsible AI system that may help people organize life, preserve memory, reflect safely, and create accessible personal worlds with user control, consent, and privacy.
