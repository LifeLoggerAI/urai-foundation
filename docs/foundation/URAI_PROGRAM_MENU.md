# URAI Foundation Program Concept Menu

Status: formation-stage concepts only

## Boundary

These concepts are possible future public-interest directions. They are not active programs, services, studies, grant-funded projects, clinical offerings, or partner commitments. No enrollment, eligibility, delivery, outcome, or funding claim should be made until governance, legal authority, safety review, qualified expertise, resources, and operating evidence exist.

## Memory Worlds for Seniors — concept

Explore whether user-controlled tools could help older adults and families organize memories, stories, routines, preferences, values, and identity continuity without making medical, dementia-treatment, diagnostic, or efficacy claims.

Prerequisites include older-adult and caregiver participation, accessibility review, consent/capacity analysis, privacy/security controls, grief/legacy safeguards, and qualified legal/ethics review.

## Veteran Life Continuity Map — concept

Explore whether a voluntary, non-clinical life-story and future-planning experience could help veterans organize goals, support systems, documents, family stories, and transitions.

This concept must not imply VA approval, treatment of PTSD/TBI, benefits advice, crisis response, or representation of veterans as a whole. Veteran and disability-community review is required.

## Cognitive Accessibility Interface — concept

Develop and test simplified, low-stimulation, readable, predictable reflection and memory-organization patterns.

The current checklist is design guidance, not proof of accessibility, cognitive benefit, medical validation, or WCAG conformance.

## User-Controlled Replay Design — concept

Define replay controls for preview, skip, blur, lock, exit, correction, deletion, redaction, provenance, uncertainty, and user confirmation before generated material enters a durable record.

## Youth Identity and Future Worlds — concept

Explore age-appropriate creative reflection and future-planning only after minor/dependent-user governance, parental/guardian boundaries, privacy, advertising/commercial limits, safety, accessibility, and qualified child-development/legal review are established.

## Accessibility-first URAI — cross-cutting standard direction

Future product guidance should address screen readers, keyboard and switch access, captions, transcripts, reduced motion, high contrast, plain language, predictable flows, accessible authentication, user-controlled timeouts, low-stimulation options, and safe non-immersive alternatives.

## Activation gate

A concept may be described as an active program only when a public record identifies:

- authorized operator and legal basis;
- governance approval;
- scope, users, location, and duration;
- qualified reviewers and conflicts;
- safety/privacy/accessibility/risk review;
- funding and sponsor disclosures;
- consent and data handling;
- complaint and incident paths;
- measurable goals without fabricated impact;
- launch date and operating evidence;
- correction, suspension, and closure process.
