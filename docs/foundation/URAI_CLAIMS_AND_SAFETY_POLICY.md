# URAI Foundation Claims and Safety Policy

## Core rule

URAI must be positioned as an AI-assisted memory, identity, reflection, accessibility, narrative-continuity, and human-development platform.

URAI must not be positioned as a medical device, diagnosis system, treatment, therapy replacement, crisis tool, or clinical predictor unless a future formal validation and regulatory pathway supports that claim.

## Allowed claims

URAI may say it helps users:

- organize memories
- preserve life stories
- reflect on life patterns
- explore identity
- visualize goals
- create personal story/world experiences
- support caregiver/family connection
- create source-backed personal replays
- track user-confirmed patterns over time
- build narrative continuity
- engage in guided self-reflection
- improve accessibility of personal reflection and memory organization

## Restricted claims

Use only with careful context and advisor/legal review:

- mental health support
- emotional regulation support
- trauma-informed
- cognitive support
- memory support
- therapeutic
- clinical
- diagnostic
- predictive
- risk detection

## Prohibited claims

Do not say URAI:

- diagnoses medical or mental health conditions
- treats trauma
- cures depression or anxiety
- treats PTSD
- treats TBI
- treats Alzheimer’s or dementia
- predicts clinical outcomes
- replaces therapy
- replaces medical care
- is VA-approved
- knows the user better than the user
- determines another person’s true emotional state
- is clinically proven without studies
- is a medical device without regulatory work
- uses hidden/private data without consent

## AI behavior rules

AI must:

- cite or identify evidence where possible
- label inference as inference
- ask permission before sensitive interpretation
- allow user correction
- allow deletion/redaction
- avoid permanent labels
- avoid diagnosing
- avoid claiming certainty about emotions
- avoid inferring another person’s inner life as fact
- avoid using hidden/deleted/vaulted data
- avoid writing to identity/memory without user confirmation

## Vulnerable population rules

For youth, older adults, people in distress, grieving users, people rebuilding life stability, or users with disability access needs:

- use extra clear consent
- use human partner oversight
- avoid intense autoplay
- avoid manipulative engagement
- provide exit controls
- provide support/referral language when needed
- keep data collection minimal
- avoid clinical claims
