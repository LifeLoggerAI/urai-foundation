# Risk Review Process

Use this process for proposed AI systems, policy changes, or deployments that may affect safety, rights, privacy, or public trust.

## 1. Intake

Document the proposal, owner, intended users, deployment context, data categories, expected benefits, and known constraints.

## 2. Initial classification

Classify the proposal as low, medium, or high risk based on:

- Potential impact on legal rights, access to resources, or personal safety.
- Use of sensitive personal data.
- Scale of deployment and affected population.
- Autonomy of the system and availability of human review.
- Misuse potential and reversibility of harms.

## 3. Evidence gathering

Collect relevant documentation, evaluations, threat models, privacy analysis, accessibility review, and stakeholder input.

## 4. Mitigation plan

For each material risk, define controls, owners, deadlines, monitoring signals, and rollback or escalation criteria.

## 5. Approval decision

Approvals should state scope, conditions, expiry or reassessment date, and unresolved risks. Rejections should state the blocking concerns and evidence needed for reconsideration.

## 6. Post-deployment monitoring

Approved systems should be monitored for incidents, user reports, drift, performance regressions, and changed legal or social context.

## 7. Reassessment triggers

Reassess when there are major model changes, new data sources, new user populations, incidents, legal changes, or credible external concerns.
