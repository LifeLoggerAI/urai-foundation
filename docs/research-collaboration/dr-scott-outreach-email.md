## Sent Email

Dear Dr. Scott,

Thank you again for taking the time to talk with me. I genuinely appreciate your openness to discussing these ideas.

I wanted to summarize what I've been building and why I believe there may be an opportunity for meaningful scientific collaboration.

The broader vision is URAI, an AI-driven platform designed to help people better understand themselves over time by integrating patterns across cognition, emotion, behavior, environment, memory, and daily life. Rather than focusing only on isolated measurements, the goal is to model how these domains interact dynamically.

One of the ideas I'm especially interested in exploring is that variability over time—not simply average scores—may provide important information about future trajectories. My hypothesis is that changes in patterns and relationships between measures may offer earlier insight into functional change than static snapshots alone. This is an idea that would need rigorous testing through well-designed scientific studies.

I also believe there may be opportunities to explore how this framework could complement research related to intrinsic capacity by capturing longitudinal changes across multiple domains of function. My hope is to develop methods that can eventually support earlier detection, personalized interventions, and a richer understanding of human health across the lifespan.

I'm not looking for someone to simply endorse these ideas. I'm looking for scientific partners who are willing to challenge them, refine them, and test them rigorously. If the evidence supports the concepts, I'd love to help build that evidence together.

If you're interested, I'd also like to discuss how you might become involved with the URAI Foundation as we build a multidisciplinary network of researchers, clinicians, engineers, and advisors focused on advancing this work responsibly.

Thank you again for your time and consideration. I look forward to continuing the conversation and learning from your expertise.

Best,
Adam
