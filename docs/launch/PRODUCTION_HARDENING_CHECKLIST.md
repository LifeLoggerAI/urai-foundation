# URAI Foundation Production Hardening Checklist

Domain: uraifoundation.org

Before production approval:

- Confirm public foundation routes exist.
- Confirm public metadata exists.
- Confirm public pages link to URAI Privacy.
- Confirm accessibility and deaf-community language is respectful and claim-safe.
- Confirm wellness language does not make medical or therapy claims.
- Confirm foundation status and donation language is legally accurate.
- Confirm research and partner forms preserve UTM/source fields.
- Confirm forms write to approved backend.
- Confirm app, Studio, Labs, and Privacy routing works.
- Run build, typecheck, tests, and QA.
- Record DNS, SSL, deploy URL, latest commit, and owner approval.
