# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in URAI Foundation spaces.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and patience.
- Welcome constructive disagreement and evidence-based critique.
- Focus on ideas, impacts, and trade-offs rather than personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality boundaries.
- Help maintain accessible and inclusive discussion spaces.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sustained disruption, sexualized attention, or knowingly sharing private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers through the repository issue process when safe, or through the private contact path listed in `SECURITY.md` for sensitive concerns.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or escalate concerns when needed to protect contributors and the integrity of the project.
