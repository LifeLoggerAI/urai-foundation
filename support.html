Support

Help strengthen public-interest AI standards and accessibility work.

URAI Foundation support helps fund open governance materials, accessibility guidance, educational resources, transparency practices, and community accountability efforts.

Ways to help

Support may include donations, sponsorship, partnerships, accessibility review collaboration, volunteer expertise, research participation, or educational outreach.

Organizations and researchers interested in collaboration can contact the Foundation directly.

How support may be used

Public-interest documentation, accessibility review, standards development, educational materials, translation work, governance tooling, community review sessions, and safer AI guidance.

Formation status

Support language should remain accurate and legally reviewed.

URAI Foundation is in active formation. Donation, sponsorship, grant, fiscal sponsorship, and tax-deductibility claims should not be published unless they accurately reflect the Foundation's current legal status.

Until those details are finalized, the site should use careful language that prioritizes transparency over urgency.

Get involved

Support, volunteer, or partner with the Foundation.

For partnership, accessibility collaboration, volunteer interest, or support inquiries, contact the Foundation directly.
