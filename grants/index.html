Current boundary

This route is an interactive workflow prototype. It stores demonstration data only in this browser. It does not transmit a real grant application, create legal eligibility, guarantee funding, or establish URAI Foundation as a grantmaker, charity, fiscal sponsor, or agent of any funder unless separately verified.

Applicant attestation

Nothing gets signed silently.

I reviewed this exact application version. To the best of my knowledge, the factual statements I am attesting to are accurate, and I understand that generated draft language is my responsibility to review before any external submission.

Direct submission to a third-party grant portal is not enabled in this prototype. A production submission adapter must be authorized for each funder and preserve the applicant's final review and sign-off.

How the production service should work

Automation does the repetition. People keep the judgment.

One verified profile

Store reusable organization, program, budget, people, document, and impact facts with source and confirmation metadata.

Provenance on every answer

Show whether an answer came from the applicant profile, an authorized document, a prior approved response, or an AI-generated draft.

Exact-version sign-off

Any edit after attestation invalidates the old approval. The applicant signs only the version they actually reviewed.
