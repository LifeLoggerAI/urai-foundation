About

Public-interest standards for technology that touches real life.

URAI Foundation is the nonprofit and public-interest layer of the URAI ecosystem. Its role is to develop practical standards, education, accessibility guidance, privacy-first practices, and community accountability for human-centered AI.

Why it exists

Powerful systems need public accountability.

AI systems increasingly shape memory, work, communication, identity, mental wellbeing, relationships, accessibility, and public trust. The Foundation exists to help ensure these systems are reviewed through clear standards rather than vague promises.

The work is intentionally grounded: privacy, consent, accessibility, safety, explainability, community benefit, and clear boundaries around what technology should and should not claim to do.

Ecosystem relationship

Separate roles, clearer trust.

URAI Labs

Builds products, prototypes, and commercial systems within the broader URAI ecosystem.

URAI Foundation

Publishes public-interest standards, ethics guidance, accessibility research, and community accountability practices.

URAI Privacy

Defines consent, data-protection, minimization, and privacy constraints that should guide sensitive systems.

The Foundation should not be used as a marketing substitute for commercial products. Its purpose is public benefit, reviewability, and trust.

Current status

In active formation.

URAI Foundation is in active formation. Public materials describe the intended mission, standards, and programs. Donation, sponsorship, grant, and tax-deductibility language should be reviewed and updated as the legal structure or fiscal sponsorship status is finalized.

Until that status is complete, the Foundation should communicate plainly, avoid overclaiming, and make clear how support will be used.
