URAI Foundation public status

URAI Foundation is the official public-interest standards and governance initiative within the URAI ecosystem.

Legal and tax boundary: This page does not establish separate incorporation, federal tax-exempt recognition, charitable-registration status, or donation deductibility. Those claims require authoritative records and will be published only after verification.

Public commitment

Legal identity, governance authority, programs, partnerships, and financial claims will be stated only when supported by governing records or independently verifiable evidence.

Ecosystem roles

Distinct functions and responsibilities

URAI Foundation

The public-interest standards and governance initiative focused on ethics, research, accountability, accessibility, and human-centered technology.

Information still awaiting authoritative publication

The Foundation initiative will publish the following only after comparison with governing records:

  • Whether a separate legal entity has been formed
  • Exact entity type and jurisdiction
  • Formation date and public filing identifier
  • Authorized directors, officers, or governing roles
  • Adopted bylaws or equivalent governing documents
  • Applicable tax-exemption or charitable-registration status
  • Donation eligibility and financial transparency records

Until those records are posted, visitors should not infer separate incorporation, 501(c)(3) recognition, registered-charity status, or tax-deductible contribution eligibility from the Foundation name or the .org domain.
