Community outreach

Technology should show up for people in the places they already live, learn, work, and connect.

URAI Foundation’s public work centers on access, practical education, community participation, responsible technology, and listening to people who are often left out of technology design.

Start a community conversationPartner with the Foundation

Current boundary: Programs, events, grants, partnerships, and services are described as active only when separately verified. This page does not create eligibility, representation, or a promise of assistance.

What community means here

Meet people with dignity, make information understandable, design for access, and leave room for communities to shape the technology that affects them.

Ways to participate

A public front door, not a wall of institutional language.

Community learning

Plain-language sessions, demonstrations, digital-literacy support, and responsible-AI education as programs are established and published.

Accessibility outreach

Work shaped around visual, haptic, cognitive, communication, mobility, and sensory access, with community review rather than assumed representation.

Local partnerships

Schools, nonprofits, community organizations, researchers, and public-interest groups can explore projects without implying a partnership before it is confirmed.

Volunteer interest

People can raise their hand to help with outreach, events, accessibility review, research support, storytelling, or community operations when roles open.

Community listening

Feedback, lived experience, concerns, and ideas can inform Foundation priorities and public standards without turning participation into data extraction.

Public accountability

Programs should publish what they are, who they serve, what was learned, what changed, and what remains uncertain.
