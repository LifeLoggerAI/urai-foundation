Terms and disclaimers

Responsible language and clear boundaries matter.

URAI Foundation materials are intended for public-interest education, governance, accessibility, ethics discussion, and responsible AI guidance.

No medical or mental-health services

URAI Foundation does not provide medical care, mental-health treatment, crisis intervention, diagnosis, legal advice, or emergency services. Educational materials discussing emotional wellbeing, accessibility, memory, or AI-supported reflection should not be treated as professional care.

No guarantee of outcomes

The Foundation should avoid exaggerated or guaranteed claims about AI capabilities, prediction, emotional understanding, behavioral interpretation, or human outcomes.

Independent judgment

Readers, researchers, contributors, donors, and partners should exercise independent judgment and consult qualified professionals where appropriate.

External links and third-party services

The website may link to GitHub, partner organizations, donation providers, accessibility resources, or other third-party services. Those services operate under their own policies and terms.

Intellectual property

Unless otherwise stated, Foundation-written standards and public-interest materials may be shared with attribution for educational, nonprofit, research, governance, and accountability purposes.

Community participation

Participation in public repositories, issue discussions, governance review, or volunteer collaboration should follow the published Code of Conduct and related governance expectations.

Formation status

The Foundation is in active formation. References to programs, partnerships, governance, support, or nonprofit positioning describe intended public-interest direction and may evolve as legal and operational structures mature.
