Accessibility

Accessibility is part of responsible design.

URAI Foundation treats accessibility as part of responsible design, documentation, and review. This is a formation-era standards commitment, not a certification claim.

Status: URAI Foundation is a public-interest standards/docs initiative in active formation. This page does not claim audit completion, certification, or live accessibility services.

Scope

Clear language, navigable structure, keyboard-aware design, readable contrast, and reviewable accessibility expectations.

Inclusive design

Public standards should consider people with different sensory, motor, cognitive, language, and access needs before deployment decisions are made.

Documentation quality

Foundation materials should be readable, structured, linkable, and usable with assistive tools wherever practical.

Review expectation

High-impact systems should document accessibility assumptions, known gaps, mitigation plans, and owner follow-up.
