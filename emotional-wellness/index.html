Emotional wellness standards

Careful systems should respect emotional context.

This page frames emotional wellness as a safety, consent, transparency, and product-review concern for human-centered technology.

Status: URAI Foundation does not provide medical care, therapy, diagnosis, crisis response, clinical support, or wellness services. This is standards documentation only. Review the canonical public-status disclosure.

Boundary

Products that touch memory, identity, grief, relationships, stress, or vulnerable moments should be reviewed with extra care.

Safety framing

High-impact emotional contexts should document risks, escalation paths, limitations, user controls, and inappropriate-use boundaries.

Consent and agency

Users should understand when AI is involved, what data may be used, and what choices they have.

No clinical claims

Public copy should avoid medical, therapeutic, diagnostic, crisis-service, or efficacy claims unless separately verified and approved.
