Deaf community considerations

Respect starts with humility, access, and review.

URAI Foundation documents standards for technology that may affect Deaf, hard-of-hearing, signing, caption-reliant, and access-dependent users.

Status: This page does not claim representation of any community, official endorsement, formal partnership, live program, or community service. It is a formation-era standards page.

Boundary

Nothing about Deaf communities should be designed from assumption alone. Standards should require evidence, consent, and affected-community review where possible.

Access-first review

Systems should consider captions, transcripts, visual alerts, signed-language contexts, timing, readability, and user control.

No representation claim

The Foundation does not speak for Deaf communities. Public language should avoid implying official voice, endorsement, or coverage.

Respectful participation

Future work should invite feedback through transparent, voluntary, and non-extractive review paths.
