Responsible AI

Responsible AI needs evidence, limits, and accountability.

URAI Foundation publishes formation-era standards for governance, transparency, review, and contestability.

Status: These are public standards and review expectations, not a certification system or guarantee about any product or organization.

Core documents

Ethical AI Principles, Transparency Framework, and Risk Review Process.

Governance

Decisions should have named owners, review categories, exceptions, and traceable records.

Transparency

Systems should state purpose, limits, oversight, and concern paths when public disclosure is safe.

Risk review

High-impact systems should classify risk, gather evidence, set controls, record decisions, and reassess after changes.
